✦ About the school

A trusted neighbourhood school in the heart of Krishanpura

Sadanand Bal Vidya Mandir Sr. Sec. School has been educating children of Panipat along Khanna Road in Krishanpura, giving families a school that's close to home and serious about learning. We teach the CBSE curriculum from Nursery through Class XII, with a strong focus on foundational Maths, Science and Languages.

Small class sizes and a disciplined, caring campus mean every student is known by name — not just a roll number. Alongside academics, we give equal weight to sport, art and the values that shape good citizens.

✦ Beyond the classroom

Activities that build the whole child

Sport, art and clubs so a report card is never the only measure of a child's year.

⚽

Sports & Athletics

Football, kabaddi, athletics and yoga with daily PT periods and an annual sports day.

🎨

Art, Craft & Music

Weekly art, craft and music classes, plus a school choir and instrumental band.

🔬

Science Lab

Hands-on science experiments in a dedicated lab for classes VI and above.

💃

Dance & Drama

Folk and classical dance troupes, plus an annual inter-house drama competition.

📚

Reading & Debate Club

Library hour every week, plus debate, elocution and quiz competitions all year round.

🌱

Eco Club

Tree plantation drives and cleanliness campaigns around Krishanpura and Khanna Road.

Typically the child's birth certificate, Aadhaar card, previous school's transfer certificate and report card, and passport-size photographs. Exact requirements are confirmed during the consultation call.
