Our Story

Founded on the belief that every child matters

Asian Public School has been a trusted name in Panipat's Sukhdev Nagar community for over a decade. Our approach is simple: know each child, challenge them appropriately, and celebrate their progress — not just their grades.

We keep our classes intentionally small so that every student gets the attention they deserve. Our teachers design lessons around real understanding, and our counsellors work closely with families to support each child's journey.
