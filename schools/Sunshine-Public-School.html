FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about admissions, curriculum and school routine at Sunshine Public School.

What curriculum does the school follow?

Sunshine Public School follows a structured academic curriculum from the primary years through senior secondary.

What is the medium of instruction?

Classes are conducted in English, with Hindi taught as a core language subject.

Where is the school located?

The campus is at 58M, Unnamed Road, Dutta Colony, Panipat Taraf Ansar, Panipat, Haryana 132105.

How do I apply for admission?

Visit the admissions office or call 89282 92537 to begin the enrolment process.

Do you provide transport facilities?

Yes, school transport routes cover Panipat and nearby areas — contact the office for route details.

What are the school timings?

School hours are from 7:45 AM to 1:45 PM for all classes, with additional activity hours on selected days.
