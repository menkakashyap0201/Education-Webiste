About Bhumika Public School

Small enough to know every child by name

We started with a simple idea: young children learn best when their teachers actually know them — their pace, their curiosity, their quiet strengths. That idea still shapes how every classroom at Bhumika runs today.

Our campus in Hari Nagar keeps class sizes manageable and staff involved beyond the timetable, so learning stays personal rather than procedural.

  • Focused academics from the early years through middle school
  • Teachers who track each child's progress individually
  • A safe, easily-supervised neighbourhood campus
Why families choose us

What a Bhumika education looks like day to day

Every choice on our campus, from class size to how the day is scheduled, is made to keep children engaged rather than just occupied.

Attentive teaching

Teachers who stay with a subject until it's understood, not just covered.

Safe, supervised campus

A compact, gated campus where staff can see and reach every classroom quickly.

Individual attention

Manageable class sizes so quieter students aren't left behind the loudest ones.

Sports & activity time

Daily physical activity built into the timetable, not treated as an extra.

Steady communication

Parents kept in the loop on progress, not just at report card time.

Values alongside academics

Discipline, courtesy, and responsibility are taught as deliberately as maths.
