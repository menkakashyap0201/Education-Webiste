About Our School

Twenty-five years of teaching Panipat's children to think for themselves

Jyotiba Phule Public School opened its doors in Sondhapur with a simple idea: a school should prepare a child for life, not just for an exam. Today we run classes I through XII on one campus, following the CBSE curriculum, with a faculty that stays with students year after year.

We keep sections small, involve parents in the everyday progress of their child, and treat sport, music and art as seriously as the board subjects — because that is what actually builds confident adults.

CBSE-affiliated since 2001

Recognised by the Haryana Board of School Education.

Experienced, long-serving faculty

Most senior teachers have taught here for over a decade.

Rooted in the local community

Generations of Panipat families have studied on this campus.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What documents are required for admission?
Birth certificate, previous school's transfer certificate and report card, Aadhaar card, and four passport-size photographs of the student.
Does the school provide transport in Panipat?
Yes, school buses cover most routes across Panipat including Sondhapur and the surrounding sectors. Route details are shared at the time of admission.
What is the medium of instruction?
Classes are conducted in English, with Hindi and Sanskrit taught as additional languages as per the CBSE syllabus.
Which streams are offered in Class XI and XII?
Science (Medical and Non-Medical), Commerce, and Humanities, each with dedicated subject teachers and lab access.
How can I book a campus visit?
Call the admissions office or fill the contact form below — visits are scheduled Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 1 PM.
