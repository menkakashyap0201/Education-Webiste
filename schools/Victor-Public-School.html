About the school

Excellence in academics, strength in values.

A CBSE-affiliated school in the heart of Panipat, focused on academics, character and all-round growth from Nursery through Class XII.

Victor Public School has been a trusted name in Panipat's education landscape, known for its structured teaching, attentive staff and a campus where every student is encouraged to do their personal best.

Our classrooms blend CBSE's academic rigour with project work, regular assessments and one-on-one attention, so students don't just clear exams — they build the habits that carry them through college and beyond.

Beyond the classroom, we run a full calendar of sports, cultural events and clubs, because we believe a well-rounded school day is what actually builds confidence.

Common questions

FAQ

Answers to what parents usually ask before joining.

Which board is the school affiliated to?+

Victor Public School follows the CBSE curriculum from Nursery through Class XII. Please confirm the current CBSE affiliation number with the school office for official records.

What classes and streams are offered?+

We offer Nursery to Class XII, with Science, Commerce and Humanities streams available at the senior secondary level.

How do I apply for admission?+

Call the school office or book a consultation through this website. Our admissions desk will schedule a campus visit and share the fee structure and required documents.

What documents are needed for admission?+

Typically the child's birth certificate, previous school's transfer certificate and report card, address proof, and passport-size photographs. Exact requirements are confirmed at the consultation.

Is transport available?+

Yes, school transport is available on select routes around Panipat. Ask the admissions desk to confirm whether your area is covered.

What is the school's contact number for queries?+
