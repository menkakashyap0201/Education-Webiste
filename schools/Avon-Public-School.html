About Avon Public School

Located in Krishna Nagar, Tehsil Camp, Panipat Taraf Ansar, Panipat, Avon Public School has been a trusted name in quality education, shaping confident, disciplined and curious learners since its founding.

We believe every child learns differently, so our teachers combine structured academics with sports, arts and hands-on activities to help students discover their strengths.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions parents ask us most often.

What classes does the school offer?+
We offer classes from Nursery to Grade 12, following the CBSE curriculum across Pre-Primary, Primary, Middle and Senior Secondary stages.
Does the school provide bus transport?+
Yes, we run GPS-tracked school buses on routes covering major areas of Panipat, with an attendant on every bus.
How do I apply for admission?+
You can visit our campus in Krishna Nagar, Tehsil Camp, Panipat Taraf Ansar, or call 094161 05007 to begin the admission process and schedule a campus tour.
Is counselling support available for students?+
Yes, our in-house counsellors support students with academic guidance, career planning and emotional wellbeing.
What facilities are available on campus?+
Our campus includes a library, smart classrooms, science labs, sports grounds, gardens, a medical room and a cafeteria.
