Our Story

Nearly two decades in Bharat Nagar

New Haryana Public School opened on Kabri Road with a straightforward promise to Panipat families: every child would be known, not just enrolled. That promise still shapes how we run the school today — from class sizes to how often teachers call home.

We follow the CBSE curriculum from Nursery through Class XII, but we treat sport, art and everyday character as seriously as the subjects that show up on a report card.

Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What documents are needed for admission?
Birth certificate, the previous school's transfer certificate and report card, an Aadhaar card, and four passport-size photographs of the student.
Does the school provide transport in Panipat?
Yes — GPS-tracked buses cover most routes across Panipat, including Bharat Nagar and Kabri Road, with an attendant on every bus. Route details are shared at the time of admission.
What is the medium of instruction?
Classes are conducted in English, with Hindi and Sanskrit taught as additional languages under the CBSE syllabus.
Which streams are offered in Class XI and XII?
Science (Medical and Non-Medical), Commerce, and Humanities, each with dedicated subject teachers and lab access.
How do I book a campus visit?
Call the admission desk at 94169 59518. Visits are scheduled Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 1 PM.
