About the school

A nurturing campus built for how children actually learn

Little Angel Senior Secondary School sits in Bandh, on the Panipat–Sonipat belt, serving families across V.P.O Bandh and the surrounding tehsil. We follow the CBSE curriculum from Nursery through Class XII, with a teaching approach that balances strong fundamentals in Maths, Science and Languages with hands-on activity, sport and creative expression.

Our classrooms stay small enough for teachers to know every child by name — their strengths, their pace, and where they need a nudge. That personal attention, alongside a genuinely safe and disciplined campus, is what most parents mention first when they talk about us.

📘

Academic rigour, gently paced

Structured CBSE curriculum with regular assessments and remedial support.

🤝

Values before everything

Discipline, respect and empathy are taught as deliberately as any subject.

🏃

Sport, art & activity every week

Every student takes part in physical education, art and a co-curricular club.

Beyond the classroom

Activities that build the whole child

A packed calendar of sport, art and clubs so that report cards are never the only measure of a child's year.

⚽

Sports & Athletics

Football, kabaddi, athletics and yoga with daily PT periods and an annual sports day.

🎨

Art, Craft & Music

Weekly art, craft and music classes, plus a school choir and instrumental band.

🔬

Science & Robotics Lab

Hands-on science experiments and a robotics club for classes VI and above.

💃

Dance & Drama

Classical and folk dance troupes, and an annual inter-house drama competition.

📚

Reading & Debate Club

Library hour every week, plus debate, elocution and quiz competitions all year round.

🌱

Eco & Community Club

Tree plantation drives, cleanliness campaigns and visits to nearby community centres.
