About the school

Gentle beginnings, joyful learning.

A play-based preschool in Friends Colony, Panipat, focused on early development, confidence and a love of learning from Playgroup through Kindergarten.

Rising Star Kids Playway School is a trusted early-learning space in Panipat, known for its warm classrooms, caring staff and a campus where every child is encouraged to explore at their own pace.

Our activities blend structured play with stories, rhymes, art and simple motor and language exercises, so children build the foundational habits — listening, sharing, curiosity — that carry them into formal schooling.

Beyond the classroom, we run a calendar of festivals, indoor and outdoor play, and parent-involvement days, because we believe early childhood is best spent learning through joy.

Common questions

FAQ

Answers to what parents usually ask before joining.

What age groups does the school accept?+

Rising Star Kids Playway School welcomes children roughly between 1.5 and 5 years of age, across Playgroup, Nursery, LKG and UKG.

What kind of learning approach do you follow?+

We follow a play-based approach, using activities, stories, rhymes and guided play to build early motor, language and social skills.

How do I apply for admission?+

Call the school office or book a consultation through this website. Our admissions desk will schedule a campus visit and share the fee structure and required documents.

What documents are needed for admission?+

Typically the child's birth certificate, a recent passport-size photograph, and address proof. Exact requirements are confirmed at the consultation.

Is pick-up and drop assistance available?+

Yes, pick-up and drop assistance is available on select routes around Panipat. Ask the admissions desk to confirm whether your area is covered.

What is the school's contact number for queries?+

You can reach the school office at 099922 11109.
