A neighbourhood school, not a factory

Shine Star sits in Bharat Nagar, off Kabri Road in Jawahar Nagar, close enough that most of our children walk to school. That closeness shapes everything. Teachers know the families. Parents can drop in. A child who has a difficult morning does not disappear into a crowd of four hundred.

We keep sections small on purpose. It means reading is heard out loud, homework is checked by hand, and a child who falls behind in maths is noticed in the same week — not at the end of the term.

The work here is ordinary and steady: clear handwriting, tables learnt properly, questions asked without fear, and the manners that make a child welcome anywhere.

A school is judged by how the quietest child in the room is treated.

Parents come to us with a simple worry — will my child be seen? In a large school it is easy for a shy child to sit at the back for eleven years and never be asked a question. We built this school around the opposite idea.

Our teachers are asked to know every child's handwriting, every child's weak subject, and every child's home situation. When a student stops doing homework, we call the parent that week — not at the end of the term. When a student does something well, we say so in front of the class.

If you are considering us, please come and sit in the corridor for a while during school hours. Watch how the children move between classes. That will tell you more than any brochure can.

Taking admission

Four steps, usually finished in a single visit to the office.

Visit or call

Come to the office between 8 am and 2 pm, or ring 073572 91731 and we will tell you what is available in your child's class.

Collect the form

Fill the admission form at the office. Bring the birth certificate, two photographs and the last report card if your child is transferring.

Meet the teacher

A short, friendly interaction so we know where your child stands. For Nursery and LKG it is simply a conversation, nothing to prepare for.

Confirm the seat

Pay the admission fee, collect the fee schedule and book list in writing, and your child can start the following Monday.

Come and see the school

The best way to judge a school is to stand in its corridor for ten minutes. You are welcome any working day.
