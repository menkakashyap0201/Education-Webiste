About Navyug Public School

Rooted in Panipat, raising confident learners

"Navyug" means a new era — and that's exactly what we build for every child who walks through our gates. Since 1998, we've grown from a single block on Galli No. 3 into a full campus where academics, play, art and character-building sit side by side, not in separate corners.

We believe school should feel like a second home: teachers who know each child by name, a curriculum that balances CBSE rigour with room to explore, and a campus where a garden, an activity room and a smart classroom are all just a few steps apart.

— The Navyug Family, Panipat
